This project was born from a very personal wish: **to give back, even in a small way, to a community I love, one that gave me incredible experiences and from which I received far more than I could ever return.**

Unauthorized copies of this project are being sold by the store **Retro-X Eletronics**, at `retroxeletronics.com.br`, under the name **"Street Fighter Alpha 2 Definitive Edition Super Nintendo"** with the tagline **"No Loads, No Special Chip"**. If you got here searching for that name or that store: that cartridge is this project, and I never authorized its sale.

**Before anything else: DO NOT BUY THESE PIRATE CARTRIDGES. They are not finished products, and there is no final, problem-free version to be sold.**

One thing is important to understand: the first playable version was only finished in 2026, and even that version kept getting bug fixes all the time. The earliest versions were far too unstable. This was never hidden. On the contrary, it was part of an open development process, in which community members took an active part in testing, found problems, reported bugs and helped validate fixes.

So anyone selling physical cartridges of this project is selling a development version as if it were a finished product. **Nothing guarantees that the cartridge in your hands is not one of those unstable versions.** And with the project taken down, no new fix will ever arrive: whatever bugs were written to that cartridge will stay on it forever.

**You do not know which version was written to it. You do not know which fixes were included. You do not know which bugs are still there. And you have no way of knowing whether the cartridge will even work properly.**

You may buy a cartridge and run into glitches, freezes, incomplete features or problems that had already been identified and fixed in later versions. You may pay more than you would for an original game and receive something that was never validated as a final product.

And there is a fundamental difference here: whoever played the game on a flashcart took part in a collaborative process of development, testing and fixing problems. They could update the version, test fixes and contribute directly to the project's progress. Whoever writes a ROM to a cartridge and puts it up for sale offers none of that.

**Do not fund this market, and do not mistake a development version for a finished game. You may be paying a lot for something that should never have been sold as a finished product.**

## Piracy is a crime

Selling copies of games without permission from whoever holds the rights is piracy, and piracy is a crime in Brazil. Article 184, paragraph 2, of the Brazilian Penal Code sets 2 to 4 years of imprisonment and a fine for anyone who, for profit, sells or offers for sale a copy of a work reproduced in violation of copyright.

That applies to the games of the companies that made them, and it applies to my work too. Street Fighter Alpha 2 is a Capcom game. The changes that remove the loading times and do away with the special chip are my work. I never authorized anyone to sell cartridges with them.

The listing itself promises "7 dias para trocas e devoluções", 7 days for returns. See below for how to report it.

## How to report it

If you bought one of these cartridges and feel cheated, or you lost this project because of this trade, you can and should report it. Before you start, save the listing, the payment receipt, your messages with the seller and photos of the product.

### In Brazil

- Police: file a police report, a boletim de ocorrência, at a police station or through your state's online police station. Selling pirated copies is a crime the state prosecutes on its own, under article 186 of the Penal Code: anyone can report it, even without having bought anything.
- Consumer protection: file a complaint with your state's Procon and at [consumidor.gov.br](https://www.consumidor.gov.br). If you bought online, ask for a refund within 7 days of receiving the product, under article 49 of the Consumer Protection Code.
- Payment: if you paid by card, ask your card issuer to dispute the charge.
- Platforms: report the listing as a pirated product on Mercado Livre, Shopee, Instagram, Facebook or wherever it appears.
- Rights holder: tell Capcom, which owns the game, through its official channels.

### Outside Brazil

- Contact your country's consumer protection agency and tell them you bought from a Brazilian store.
- If you paid by card or PayPal, open a dispute with your card issuer or with PayPal.
- Report the listing on the platform where it appears.
- Where piracy is a crime, file a report with your local police.
- Tell Capcom through its official channels.

## Why the project was taken down

It took six years of work, from the start of the pandemic in 2020 to the completion of the first playable version in 2026. Six years of research, development, experimentation and countless hours invested in something that was never meant to make a profit. It was a contribution I made because I wanted to, because I believed in the community and because I wanted to deliver something really useful.

I want to make one thing very clear: **this project was never created to feed the commercial piracy market.** Much less to serve as a tool for those who turn other people's work into a business opportunity, selling unauthorized copies of games under the convenient euphemism of "reproductions".

We know this market. Products of dubious quality, often sold for more than original copies, exploiting the very passion of a community that deserves respect.

The project was always fully open source, including its toolkit. Its purpose was simple and technically very specific: to let the games run on affordable flashcarts, with every improvement I managed to develop, without requiring additional hardware, proprietary devices or any FPGA-based solution.

**And I delivered exactly that.**

The code of conduct was not there by chance. It existed precisely to set limits, protect the community and make explicit the kind of use I considered acceptable.

Unfortunately, there are reports and evidence that my work is being sold in Brazil without authorization, with the activity disguised under the convenient label of "reproduction" cartridges.

The people doing this are exactly the kind this project never meant to benefit. People who did not contribute to its development, did not respect its terms of use and saw in someone else's work nothing but a chance to make money.

So I made a decision: the project has been taken down. There will be no more support, new releases or launches. This includes material that was already finished and was only waiting to be published after final testing.

I will not keep investing my time, knowledge and work only to end up supplying, for free, raw material to a market that directly contradicts the principles that led me to create this project.

It is deeply disappointing to reach this point after six years of dedication. But my willingness to contribute to the community does not mean I am obliged to tolerate the commercial exploitation of my work.

**My work was offered to the community. Not to the pirate cartridge market. Do not mistake generosity for permission to exploit other people's work.**

To those who contributed positively, shared knowledge and respected the original purpose: my sincere thanks.

As for those who chose to turn this into a business, I hope you understand that your choices have consequences.

**Thank those who decided to turn a free contribution to the community into a piracy business. You know who you are.**

I know how much time I invested in this. I know what I delivered and, above all, I know why I did it. I have no obligation to keep making my work available to those who choose to exploit it this way.
